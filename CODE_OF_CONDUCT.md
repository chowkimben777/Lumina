# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Lumina to be a welcoming project for contributors with different levels of experience and different backgrounds.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Make room for questions and learning.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of project discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project community.
